🎥 Jitsi Video Streaming Demo

Test streaming any video into a Jitsi meeting

🌐 Network Access: http://192.168.74.69/LaunchCloudLabs/Event-Horizon/EventHorizon/video-stream-demo.html

Meeting Controls

Video Streaming

How to use:
  1. Enter a room name (any name works)
  2. Click "Start Meeting"
  3. Either load placeholder.mp4 or select a video file
  4. Click "Stream Selected Video"
  5. The video appears as your camera feed!

Share this meeting:

Share this link with others on your network. They'll join the same room and see your video stream.
